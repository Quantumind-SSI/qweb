QuantumindSSI is built on a radical premise: we collect zero data about you. Your information never leaves your device. Your privacy is not a feature: it's the foundation.

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

QuantumindSSI operates on the principle of Sovereign AI (Pillar 05). This means all data processing occurs on your device. We literally cannot access your data; even if compelled by legal order: because we never possess it.

2. What This Means

2.1 No Cloud Sync

Your VICTRON device does not sync with cloud servers. All AI models, data, and processing happen locally on your hardware.

2.2 No Telemetry

We don't collect crash reports, usage statistics, or performance metrics. Our systems are designed to be completely silent.

2.3 No Accounts Required

You can use QuantumindSSI products without creating an account, providing an email, or sharing any personal information.

3. Biometric Data (VICTRON AIO)

VICTRON AIO includes iris recognition, palm vein scanning, and optional DNA verification. This data is:

  • Stored exclusively in your device's secure enclave
  • Never transmitted to any server
  • Encrypted with your device's unique hardware key
  • Deleted permanently when you factory reset the device

4. Website Privacy

4.1 What We Track

When you browse quantumindssi.com, we collect:

  • Nothing. No analytics. No tracking pixels. No cookies.

4.2 Contact Forms

If you choose to contact us via email or contact form, we retain your message only as long as necessary to respond. We do not add you to mailing lists or marketing databases.

5. Purchase Information

When you purchase a QuantumindSSI product, our payment processor collects necessary transaction data. We retain only:

  • Order number and product purchased
  • Shipping address (for hardware delivery)
  • Email address (for order confirmation only)

This information is used solely for order fulfillment and warranty support. We do not sell or share this data.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you (answer: none)
  • Request deletion of your data (unnecessary: we have none)
  • Export your data (only from your own device)
  • Opt out of tracking (already opted out by default)

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions: admin@quantumindssi.com

Our Promise: If we ever change this privacy policy to collect more data, we will require explicit opt-in from every existing customer. Your current zero-data experience will never change without your consent.
