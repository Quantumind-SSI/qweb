01 · World Models

World Models

Reasons from continuously-updating causal world representations; not token prediction. The cognitive core persists, generalises, and grounds decisions in structured reality.

Cross-Domain Transfer

The Phase-Lock Bridge detects phase-coherent attractors across domains, enabling zero-shot knowledge transfer without retraining. Intelligence that compounds across disciplines.

03 · Goal-Directed Agency

Goal-Directed Agency

Embodied affordance modelling targets Pearl's Rung 3; counterfactual reasoning. The system doesn't just predict; it plans, intervenes, and evaluates consequences.

Recursive Self-Model

A second-order attractor system that models its own cognitive states, broadcast globally across all modules. The foundation for genuine machine metacognition.
